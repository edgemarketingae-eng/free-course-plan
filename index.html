Software & cost

Three ways to run the same flow. We recommend starting with what we already have and moving to Build our own within 60 days. Ad spend and WhatsApp message fees are extra in every plan.

How to keep WhatsApp costs near zero

Run batch groups from the free app

Announcements, reminders and class links inside the WhatsApp Community are free. Use the paid API only for 1-to-1 automation.

Get leads through Click-to-WhatsApp ads

When a lead messages us from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, replies in the free entry window cost nothing (72 hours, per Meta partners).

Reply fast, inside 24 hours

From October 2026 each number gets 1,000 free service replies a month. Templates sent outside the chat window are charged.

Alert sales reps on Telegram

A Telegram bot is free. Alerting reps there instead of by WhatsApp template saves a message fee per lead.

Teach on our Workspace Meet

Free Zoom stops group calls at 40 minutes and free Gmail Meet at 60. Our Workspace Meet avoids both limits, so we need no Zoom licence.

Odoo only for real clients

Free leads stay in Sheets and MailerLite. Only funded premium students go into Odoo, so the CRM stays clean and sales sees real clients.

Development option · least cost long term

Build our own system

One free automation server connects everything. We pay Meta directly for WhatsApp, with no monthly fee to a reseller. One developer can build it in about 3–4 weeks.
