What happens at each step

The steps explained

01

Leads for the free course

We bring people into the free course from our own social pages and from paid ads. Every new lead gets a welcome message within a minute.

Team does
  • Post free-class reels and stories every day
  • Run paid ads each week before a batch
  • Note where every lead came from
What we post
  • “5 days. Free. Live with a real trader.”
  • Short tips that show how we teach
  • Real student stories (with permission)
02

Add to the general WhatsApp community

Each lead joins the WhatsApp community and the group for the next batch. We open 4 groups a month, one per week, and run at least 3 full batches.

Team does
  • Open a new batch group every week
  • Send each lead the right group link
  • Remind anyone who hasn’t joined
What we post
  • Mentor welcome video
  • Group rules and class schedule poster
  • “Turn on notifications” reminder
03

Classes, product marketing & content

We teach the free classes live and fill the group with content that builds trust and excitement. Each day the student gets something useful.

Team does
  • Teach the free classes live, same time every day
  • Follow the posting plan for the batch
  • Share the recording with anyone who missed it
What we post
  • Class hype videos before each class
  • Posters and myth-vs-fact posts
  • “What we give today” free gift
  • Emotional posts: why most traders fail, and our way
04

Class & premium community announcement

On the last free class we announce the premium community and what students get inside it. Students unlock it by opening a broker account and depositing $50.

Team does
  • Announce premium live in the final class
  • Post the announcement in the group with a button
  • Send countdown reminders before the offer ends
What we post
  • Premium community tour video
  • Offer poster with the deadline
  • Common questions answered
05

Automated lead split to the sales team

Students tap the WhatsApp button and are sent to a sales team member automatically, shared evenly. The sales team helps them open a broker account and make the $50 deposit.

Team does
  • Reply to every student quickly, within 15 minutes
  • Guide them through account opening step by step
  • Confirm the $50 deposit, then give premium access
What we say
  • A friendly intro voice note from the sales person
  • A simple “how to open your account” guide
  • Clear answers on deposit and withdrawal
6A

Retention & deposit campaigns

We stay close to every client for the long term and run campaigns that keep them active and growing their account.

Team does
  • Weekly live market session for clients
  • A new campaign every month
  • Check in on day 7, 30, 60 and 90
  • Reach out to anyone who goes quiet
6B

Pitch the course upgrade

We invite funded students to upgrade to the full premium course with a mentor.

Team does
  • Give one free preview of the premium course
  • Share the upgrade offer a few days after joining
  • Offer a 1-to-1 call to plan their learning
  • Keep non-buyers in the retention track
