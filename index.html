The 4 automations to build first
Auto 1

Lead → batch invite. New CRM lead → pick next Monday's batch link → send template → tag. Retry in 6 h.

Auto 2

Reminders + attendance. 24 h / 1 h / 10 min. Zoom report → CRM. Replay to no-shows.

Auto 3

Claim seat → rep. 3 bot questions, round-robin, rep alert, 15-min timer, escalate.

Auto 4

Deposit → onboarding. IB export ≥ $50 → “Funded” → premium invite + sequences.

Free course syllabus
5 live sessions · 60–75 min

Forex Foundations

Day 1
How forex really worksPairs, pips, lots, sessions in GST, who moves price, why beginners fail. Homework: open a demo.
Day 2
Reading a chartCandles, timeframes, trend vs range, support & resistance. Homework: mark 3 zones.
Day 3
Risk management first1% rule, stop loss, risk:reward, position sizing. Gift: Edge risk sheet.
Day 4
One setup + the newsRule-based S/R setup on demo. Economic calendar and red-folder events.
Day 5
Psychology & roadmapTilt, journaling, a 90-day plan. Premium course + community reveal.
Deliverables

Deck per day, homework card, one gift, replay links, completion certificate.

Edge Trader Program

Week 1
