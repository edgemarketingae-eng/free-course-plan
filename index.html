Split leads equally

Sales headEvery new lead

Leads are shared evenly across Sales person 1 to 4 in rotation, so every sales person gets the same number and the same mix of sources. Each lead has exactly one owner from here to the upgrade.

How it works
  • Round-robin: lead 1 → SP1, lead 2 → SP2, and so on
  • Same owner for the whole journey
  • If a sales person is off, their share goes to the next in line
Sales head checks
  • Daily count per sales person is equal
  • No lead is unassigned for more than one hour
  • Reassign leads that get no reply in 24 hours
Done when
  • Lead shows an owner in the sheet / CRM
  • Owner has been notified
Watch:Leads per sales personUnassigned leadsTime to assign

Sales person 1–4: save number, bond, 2-minute conversation

Sales teamWithin 24 hours

The first personal touch. The sales person saves the lead's number (so our status updates reach them), introduces themselves and has a short, friendly 2-minute chat. This is about trust, not selling.

Steps
  • Save the number as "Name – Batch – Source"
  • Send a voice note or short call introducing yourself
  • Ask about their trading experience and goal
  • Note their level: beginner, some experience, active
Bond builders
  • Use their name, answer every question
  • Ask them to save your number back
  • Tell them you're their contact for the whole course
Avoid
  • Talking about deposits or accounts yet
  • Long messages or copy-paste blocks
  • Any promise of profit
Opening message

"Hi [Name], I'm [Sales person] from Edge Trading Academy. Welcome to the free course! I'll be your contact the whole way through. Quick question: have you traded before, or is this your first time?"

Sales call: account opened, deposit help, broker support

Sales teamBetween class 3 and 4

Each sales person calls their own students after class 3. If the account is open, they help with the deposit. If something is stuck, they solve it with broker support.

Call checklist
  • Account opened and verified?
  • Deposit method chosen and tried?
  • Any problem with documents or payment?
Broker support
  • Escalate verification or payment issues
  • Share the broker's help steps
  • Call back once it's fixed
Done when
  • Deposit made, or
  • A clear next step and date is agreed
Call opener

"Hi [Name], it's [Sales person]. Saw you opened your account in class 3, great work. Want me to walk you through the deposit now so you're ready for class 4?"

Sales person 1–4: class attended, account created, weekly follow-up

Sales teamEvery week

Each sales person keeps the same students they started with. Every week they check in personally, make sure the student attended class and their account is active, and solve any problem early.

Weekly check
  • Attended this week's session?
  • Account active and funded?
  • Any question or problem?
Sales person does
  • One personal message or call per student
  • Send the recording if they missed the session
  • Log the check-in in the sheet
Red flags
  • No reply for 7 days
  • Withdrawal request
  • Frustration after a loss
Weekly check-in

"Hi [Name], quick check-in from [Sales person]. How did this week go? Did you catch the live session? Anything I can help with?"
