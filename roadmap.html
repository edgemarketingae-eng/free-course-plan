Who owns what

Checkpoints per phase

Phase 1 · Sales team

First contact

  • Equal share of new leads for each of the 4 sales people
  • Save the number and start a short, friendly 2-minute chat
  • Every lead added to the Growth Hub Community Class
Phase 2 · Mentor + sales

Free course

  • Account opening shown live in class 3
  • Sales call for anyone who opened an account but hasn't deposited
  • Deposit confirmed in class 4, final check in class 5
Phase 3 · Sales team

Inner Circle

  • Each student checked: class attended, account created
  • Weekly follow-up plus a daily status on deposits
  • After two weeks: offer Premium Course, Wealth Circle or F1 Club
Phase 4 · Marketing

Content loop

  • Collect student profits and testimonials from the Inner Circle
  • Publish Basith videos and proof content for the free class
  • Track how many new Growth Hub leads each piece brings in
